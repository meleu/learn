# Teaching coworkers is a first-class goal, not a side effect

The user will introduce Rego and conftest to their team, and said so before any technical requirement. A recipe that works is not enough: it must survive being repeated by someone else and defended under questioning.

**Implications**: lessons (a) prefer whiteboard-portable explanations over docs-dependent ones and (b) flag where newcomer intuition breaks, since those are the questions the user will get. L1 set the pattern: a "flag this one for your coworkers" callout on undefined-vs-false, and a decision-vs-enforcement framing to repeat verbatim. Rollout strategy (introducing policy gates without resentment) is in mission scope though not Rego; `RESOURCES.md` lists it as a gap.
